Models to benchmark

Efficientdet-lite0

Developed by Google, EfficientDet-Lite0 is built purely for Object Detection.

It belongs to a family of models optimized to run on resource-constrained devices. Its job is straightforward: you feed it an image, and it returns bounding boxes around the objects it recognizes, along with what they are (e.g., person, car, dog) and a confidence score.

SmolVLM

It actually understands the image. Created by Hugging Face, SmolVLM is a Vision-Language Model. This means it processes text and images simultaneously. Instead of drawing boxes, it chats with you about what it sees.
